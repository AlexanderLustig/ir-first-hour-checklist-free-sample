Two CSV files open in Excel or Google Sheets (Google Sheets has not been tested). Not tested: LibreOffice, Excel on the web, Excel's Accessibility Checker.
Sample_Risk_Register_Brightwater_FREE_SAMPLE_DATA.csv: filled example, header in row 1, data only so you can sort and filter.
Risk_Register_BLANK_TEMPLATE.csv: header row only. In your spreadsheet set Score = Likelihood * Impact, and Rating = High at 15 or more, Medium at 8 to 14, Low under 8.
The CSV files hold no formulas and no validation; scores in them are pre-computed values. For built-in formulas, dropdown validation and a Settings sheet for the rating thresholds, use Risk_Register_Template_and_Sample_FREE.xlsx instead.
Formulas for row 2 (Excel; Google Sheets untested): G2 = E2*F2 and H2 = IF(G2>=15,"High",IF(G2>=8,"Medium","Low")). Copy down.
Allowed values: Likelihood and Impact are whole numbers 1 to 5. Status is Open, In progress, Done or Accepted. CSF 2.0 category is an ID such as PR.AA, GV.RR or RS.MA.
