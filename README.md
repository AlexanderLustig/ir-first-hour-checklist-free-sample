# Incident Response First-Hour Checklist and Mini Tabletop (free sample)

A printable 6 page checklist for the first hour after something goes wrong at a small firm (5 to 50 people, no security team), plus a 30 minute practice drill: one made-up scenario with four short injects. The checklist covers hijacked email or cloud accounts, payment fraud, ransomware and lost or stolen devices, and has a contact sheet to fill in before you need it.

Also included: a sample risk register for a made-up 40 person accounting firm, as a Word file with a blank register page, an Excel file (Sample and Blank sheets, working formulas), and two CSV files (filled sample, blank).
